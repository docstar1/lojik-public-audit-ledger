How sealed bucket tracking works, step by step

StepStageWhat happens
1Freeze the bucketAt the cohort date we write down every single name we ranked, from rank 1 to the bottom of the universe, and the Top-10% cut line. That list is complete before any outcome is known.
2Hash the listThe frozen list is reduced to a SHA-256 fingerprint. Once that fingerprint is published, a single character of the list cannot be changed without the hash stopping recomputing.
3Wait for the window to closeNothing is graded until the three-year forward window has closed. No early exit is pulled forward and no late exit is allowed in, so the result cannot be massaged.
4Grade every pick, hit or missEvery single pick is graded: hits and misses together. We publish the full bucket -- hits, misses, winners missed outside the bucket and the share of all market exits -- so the miss rate is visible next to the hit rate.
